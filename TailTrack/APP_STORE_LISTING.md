## Description
TailTrack is a flight tracker and logbook for pilots who fly their own airplane, a club plane, or a rental. Type in a tail number and TailTrack follows it, spots the takeoff and the landing, and builds your logbook for you.

TRACK YOUR AIRPLANE
• Follow any aircraft by tail number on a live map
• Altitude, groundspeed, vertical speed, distance flown and ETA
• Takeoffs, landings, touch-and-goes and stops along the way are detected automatically
• Live Activities show your flight on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

YOUR LOGBOOK, ON BOARD
• Every flight is saved with its map, altitude profile, stats and notes
• Replay any flight and scrub through it
• Add past flights by hand, and TailTrack can look up the real flight path (Pro)
• Add photos to each flight
• See the airports you've visited, hour goals and achievements

WEATHER AND RADIO
• METAR and TAF for your route
• Digital ATIS, decoded into plain language where airports publish one
• Airport frequencies and a radio log for each flight
• Play live airband audio from a receiver you run yourself

TAILTRACK PRO (optional)
• Unlimited aircraft
• Crew Mode: follow airline flights by flight number
• Logbook page scanning
• Pilot stats and personal records
• GPX and CSV export, and shareable flight cards
• Satellite maps
• Accent colors and alternate app icons
• Past flight paths from the ADS-B archives

Pro is $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year, each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $49.99 once for Lifetime. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your profile, aircraft and logbook stay on your device. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

IMPORTANT
TailTrack is for logging and general information only. It is not a navigation, traffic or weather service and must not be used for flight decisions. Coverage and accuracy depend on community data and can be delayed or incomplete. Always use official sources and your own judgment as pilot in command.

## App Review Notes
TailTrack is a flight tracker and logbook for general-aviation pilots. It uses public ADS-B data (adsb.lol), aviation weather (aviationweather.gov) and digital ATIS (datis.clowd.io).

No login is needed. The account on first launch is a local pilot profile stored only on the device. Sign in with Apple is optional. Profile > Delete Account removes it.

To test live tracking: Fly tab, enter the tail number of an aircraft that is currently flying. Private aircraft are often on the ground, so the easiest test is Crew Mode (Pro), which follows any airline flight by flight number.

To test past flights: Logbook > + > Add Past Flight, then open the flight and tap "Find the flight path" (Pro).

Settings > My receivers plays live airband audio from a radio receiver the pilot runs themselves (an Icecast/HTTP stream). It keeps playing with the screen locked, like a radio app. You will not have a receiver to test it.

TailTrack is for logging and information only. It is not a navigation or traffic tool, and says so in the app.
